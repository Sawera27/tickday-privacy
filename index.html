Privacy Policy for TickDay

Effective date: November 25, 2026

TickDay (the "App") is a to-do checklist app published by Sa'Al ("we", "us", "the developer"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the App.

Summary

TickDay does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

The tasks, notes, checklist items, due dates, categories and settings you create are stored locally in the App's private storage on your device. We cannot see or access this information.

If you use Android's built-in backup (such as Google backup or device-to-device transfer), Android may copy this local data as part of your device backup. That process is handled by your device and Google under your own account and settings, not by us.

Information we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera access
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind

The App does not request the Internet permission and cannot send data off your device.

Permissions the App uses

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used only to show the task reminders you choose to turn on. The App asks for this permission only when you enable a reminder. You can revoke it at any time in your device settings.
  • Receive boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): used only to restore your scheduled reminders after your device restarts.

Third parties

The App contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party services that collect data.

Children's privacy

The App is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Data deletion

Because all data is stored on your device, you control it completely. You can delete it at any time by deleting tasks in the App, using Settings > Clear completed tasks, clearing the App's storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the App.

Data security

Your data is kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot access under normal Android security rules. Since no data is sent anywhere, there is no transmission of your information to protect.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
